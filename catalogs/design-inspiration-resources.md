# UI design inspiration and reference resources

Added 2026-10-27 from links supplied by the repository owner. This list is a discovery aid, not an endorsement, accessibility audit, or grant of permission to reuse third-party work. Services may change, require accounts or payment, or impose usage restrictions. Verify the current source, access conditions, attribution, license/copyright, and suitability before use. Do not treat screenshots, designs, or flows as code or as validated product requirements.
